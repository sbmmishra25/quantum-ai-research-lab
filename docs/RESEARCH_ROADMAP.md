# Quantum AI Research Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundations

- Qubits, states, gates, measurement
- Quantum circuits and entanglement
- Quantum information concepts
- Basic quantum algorithms

## Phase 2 — NISQ and variational computing

- Parameterized quantum circuits
- Variational Quantum Eigensolver
- Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm
- Noise-aware experimentation

## Phase 3 — Quantum Machine Learning

- Quantum feature maps
- Quantum kernels
- Variational quantum classifiers
- Quantum neural-network architectures
- Hybrid quantum-classical training

## Phase 4 — Quantum-enhanced optimization

- Combinatorial optimization
- Scheduling and routing
- Portfolio and resource allocation formulations
- Classical versus quantum optimization baselines

## Phase 5 — Trustworthy Quantum AI

- Explainability
- Robustness and noise sensitivity
- Calibration and uncertainty
- Reproducibility
- Resource-aware evaluation

## Phase 6 — Autonomous systems

- Quantum-assisted decision making
- Agentic AI with optimization components
- Hybrid AI/quantum planning
- Benchmarking autonomous decision pipelines

## Research discipline

Every experiment should document the problem formulation, dataset or synthetic generator, classical baseline, quantum circuit, training configuration, random seed, metrics, computational resources, limitations, and reproducibility steps.
